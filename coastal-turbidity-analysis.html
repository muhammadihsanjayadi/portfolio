Coastal Turbidity Analysis

Coastal Turbidity Change in Waters Adjacent to Indonesia Morowali Industrial Park

PLATFORM: ArcGIS Pro LANGUAGE: Python (ArcPy)

REGION

Central Sulawesi, Indonesia

DATA

Sentinel-2 MSI Level-2A

OBSERVATION DATES

2 April 2016 & 6 April 2021

Project Overview

Located on the coast of Central Sulawesi, the Indonesia Morowali Industrial Park underwent rapid expansion during the study period following the start of nickel pig iron production in 2015. Industrial development and land disturbance across Morowali's erosion-prone lateritic landscape have raised concerns about sediment runoff into nearby coastal ecosystems. This project evaluates changes in the optical turbidity signal of adjacent coastal waters by comparing Sentinel-2 Multispectral Instrument imagery acquired on 2 April 2016 and 6 April 2021.

The image-processing and change-analysis workflow was automated using ArcPy. The script applies Sentinel-2 Scene Classification Layer masking, delineates water using the Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI), intersects the water masks from the two dates to isolate persistent water, calculates the Normalized Difference Turbidity Index (NDTI), and measures later-date minus earlier-date NDTI change. The final cartographic layout was produced separately in ArcGIS Pro using the analysis outputs. The results identify widespread positive NDTI change, with the strongest increases concentrated along sections of shoreline adjacent to the industrial park.

Workflow

01

SCL Masking

Remove unsuitable and obscured Sentinel-2 observations.

02

NDWI

Delineate water using green and near-infrared spectral values.

03

Persistent Water

Intersect water masks from the two observation dates.

04

NDTI

Calculate the turbidity index within identified water pixels.

05

Change Analysis

Calculate later-date NDTI minus earlier-date NDTI.
