Project Overview

Global Snow Persistence Explorer is an interactive Google Earth Engine application for exploring annual snow persistence worldwide using ERA5-Land data. Daily snow conditions are aggregated by year to characterize the proportion of the year during which snow is present, allowing long-term spatial patterns in snow persistence to be explored across complete years from 1951 to 2025.

Annual persistence is classified into four zones representing little or no snow, intermittent snow, seasonal snow, and persistent snow. The application also calculates global area by persistence zone and includes an interactive location inspector for examining snow-covered days, annual persistence, monthly mean snow water equivalent, and glacier or ice-cap context at selected locations.
